INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

Check the invoice you received.

Use the supplier ENS identity you already know and the actual received PDF. No wallet connection is needed.

This application verifies invoices from the registered-name smart contract only. Earlier invoice deployments are not supported here.

Enter the supplier’s ENS name shown on the invoice. Independently confirm that it belongs to the supplier you expect through a trusted contact. The invoice link does not select it for you.

Copy the Invoice Authenticity Code from the PDF or its verification link. This is the full invoice ENS name, not the invoice number or supplier name.

Current revision, original authorization and current receiving policy are separate checks. Cancellation is not supported by this smart contract.
